PixelFlux offline gazetteer
===========================

Place names and coordinates are derived from the GeoNames geographical database.

  Source:  https://www.geonames.org/
  Dataset: cities15000.txt and countryInfo.txt
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)
           https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Contains 34079 populated places across 244 countries.
Rebuild with: python tools/build_gazetteer.py

This attribution must ship with any distribution of PixelFlux.
